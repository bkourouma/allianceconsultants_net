## The world in one paragraph
The offer is a **carnet de coupons** — a wallet of official service coupons. White coupon stock with a heavy 2px navy rule on a cool paper ground; each solution is a numbered coupon; commitments are **stamped** (red rubber stamp, rotated −4…−6°, slightly imperfect); the primary action is a **detachable stub** (red). Official-document trust (stamps, perforations/dashed tear lines, carbon-copy numerals) — calm, premium, never busy, never "travel agency" (NO planes, globes, suitcases, boarding-pass gate/seat jargon, route arcs beyond the single hero coupon's small Abidjan → Afrique francophone arc).

Tokens (Tailwind utilities): `bg|text|border-ink` (carrier navy #0f2a5a), `ink-soft`, `ink-mute`, `bg-enamel` (cool paper ground #eef0f5), `bg-panel` (white coupon stock), `border-rule`, `bg-night`/`bg-night-2` (deep navy for rare dark panels), `bg-red`/`text-red`/`bg-red-dk` (#d6342a: ONLY primary action stubs, stamps and active/selected marks — never decoration), `text-carbon` (#5b4aa8: filled figures such as coupon numerals only). Product colours are small coupon tabs/chips only: scope with `className="line-medicpro"` (sets `--accent`, `--on-accent`) or `style={lineStyle("medicpro")}`; use `bg-[var(--accent)] text-[var(--on-accent)]` for a chip, never for a full-bleed field. `.line-academie` = carbon violet (formations).
Materials (globals.css): `.coupon` (white, 2px navy border, 4px radius, 3px navy base shadow), `.coupon-stub` (dashed left tear), `.tear` (dashed horizontal perforation), `.stamp` (red stamp), `.carbon`, `.t-field` (mono caption). Type: `.t-display .t-h2` = Barlow Condensed 800 **italic**, tight; `.t-h3` Barlow Condensed 700; text = Barlow; `font-mono` (Chivo Mono) for coupon field captions, nav, stamp labels. `.t-lead .t-body .t-num .wrap`, easing `ease-[var(--ease-out-expo)]`.
Layout: `<div className="wrap">`. Vertical rhythm generous (sections 5–8rem); coupons are separated by whitespace, not nested.

Voice: French, vouvoiement, specific and direct; warmth/fun comes from interaction and craft (stamp press, coupon lift, tear), not jokes.

8. Same visual language across the site — every page feels like pages of the same carnet as the hero.
