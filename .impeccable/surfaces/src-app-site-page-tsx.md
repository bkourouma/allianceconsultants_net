## Direction contract

THESIS: Alliance's offer is a carnet of official coupons: each solution a numbered coupon, each commitment stamped, nothing disappears, it is "validé" or "classé". Official-document trust (stamps, perforations, carbon figures) replaces the old transit-diagram busyness. Refuses: the metro map, equal-size card grids with icon+heading+text, generic SaaS blue-on-white, decorative gradients, travel-agency clichés (no planes, globes, suitcases).
OWN-WORLD: Carrier-navy #0f2a5a ink and heavy 2px coupon rules, signal red #d6342a (stamps, the primary action stub, active states), carbon-copy purple #5b4aa8 only for filled figures (coupon numerals), crisp white coupon stock #ffffff on a cool paper ground #eef0f5. The six products keep their accent chips (docupro #1457f5, medicpro #098240, cliniquepro #00789a, immotopia #f26a1b, annonces #e0245e, école #f5b800) as small coupon colour tabs only. Type: Barlow Condensed display (italic 800 headlines, tight, large), Barlow text, Chivo Mono for coupon field captions/nav/stamp labels (mono uppercase tracked allowed INSIDE coupons and controls as field captions; never as an eyebrow above a section heading). Geometry: 2px navy borders, 4px radii, perforated dashed separators, notched stub edges, hairline ruled field boxes, rubber-stamp marks rotated -4°…-6° with slightly imperfect ink. Recognizable with content removed: white coupons with heavy navy border, a dashed tear line and a red stamp on cool paper.
STORY: In seconds the visitor reads an established firm (since 2003, Abidjan), the new headline (software + AI + automation consulting), sees six products as six numbered coupons, picks one (it lifts and opens), believes through specifics and calm authority, and tears off the stub: Demander une démo, stamped.
