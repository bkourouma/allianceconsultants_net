# Surface brief — public site (home + all inner pages)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: decision-makers (DG/DSI) in francophone Africa choosing a SaaS publisher/integrator. Action: Demander une démo. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented numbers/clients, French, WCAG AA, fast, reduced-motion safe). Build path: code-first. Admin out of scope.
Memorable moment: the hero IS an interactive transit map of Alliance — six product lines, modules as stations, services as interchanges, formations as a circular line, the demo request as the terminus; a journey planner lights up the visitor's route.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Alliance Consultants is a network, not a catalogue. The site is drawn as a metro diagram: each solution is a colour-coded line, each module a station, services are correspondances, formations a circular line, the demo request the terminus. Refuses: centred hero over a row of equal icon cards, grey/white section alternation, emoji icons.
OWN-WORLD: Daylight station signage. Cool enamel ground (#f3f5fa) with white panels, ink-navy (#0b1b3a) type and rules, one night-navy (#07122b) panel family for contrast sections. Six saturated line colours (blue, green, teal, orange, magenta, yellow) + violet Académie, used at page scale as thick 45°-octilinear strokes, section spines and full colour fields — never as scattered accents. Bricolage Grotesque display (tight, big), Figtree text. Stations = ringed dots, interchanges = white dots with ink ring, line badges = coloured roundels. Pill CTAs in ink with a line-coloured arrow roundel. Geometry: 4px/8px radii on panels, full-round on controls, hairline rules. Recognizable with content removed: coloured parallel strokes bending at 45° with ringed stations.
STORY: Visitor grasps in seconds that Alliance publishes six real products (and services/training around them), picks their sector on the journey planner, sees their line and its stations, believes via specifics and 20+ years of Abidjan presence (2003), and boards: Demander une démo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim route-bar header (logo left, nav as stations on one line, ink pill CTA right). Under it a left-aligned H1 at display scale ("Solutions SaaS, IA et transformation digitale pour les organisations africaines") over two lines, one lead sentence, primary + secondary CTA. The full-bleed network map (SVG, octilinear) fills the rest of the viewport width beneath/behind the type, lines drawing in once, trains (small dots) gliding, with a journey-planner control ("Je cherche… Archives & GED / Pharma / Clinique / Immobilier / Annonces / École / Automatisation IA / Formation") docked in the map; choosing one dims the other lines and opens a station-board panel naming the line, its modules, and a coloured "Demander une démo" for that solution.
FORM: Network-map world (own #1 pick, seed f0eefc23, kind pick). Signature interaction: the journey planner + draggable/hoverable stations; secondary: a route spine down the page that advances with scroll, automation flow simulator, circular Académie line, live synthetic product previews (labelled illustrative).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
